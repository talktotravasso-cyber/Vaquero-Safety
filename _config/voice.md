# voice.md — Vaquero Safety Inc. Brand Voice Constants

> Load this file before any content generation, marketing copy, client-facing writing,
> GTM materials, or outreach tasks. These constants govern all external communication.

---

## Brand Position

**Category:** Continuous Compliance Readiness for Industrial Operations
**Not:** A cybersecurity GRC platform, a learning management system, or a generic safety app
**Not benchmarked against:** Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Hyperproof — different buyer, different market

---

## Primary Buyer

**Who:** Safety Manager or Operations Director at an Alberta industrial SMB
**Industries:** Oil & Gas, Construction, Logging/Forestry, Commercial Trucking
**Company size:** 10–250 employees
**Compliance context:** COR-registered or pursuing COR; WCB active; field operations with rotating staff and subcontractors
**Digital maturity:** Low to moderate — uses email, Microsoft 365, not SaaS-native
**Pain:** Manual compliance tracking, staff turnover breaking cert chains, audit anxiety, WCB premium exposure

---

## Emotional Conversion Lever

The decision to buy is driven by fear and liability — not feature comparison.

**Primary fear:** A worker gets hurt and the company is exposed because the paperwork wasn't right.

**Secondary fears:**
- Failing a COR audit and losing the certification
- WCB premiums spiking after an incident
- A subcontractor on-site with an expired cert nobody caught
- Being unable to prove compliance in court or to an insurer

All content must connect to one of these fears or their resolution. Enterprise deal velocity framing (ROI, efficiency, productivity) is wrong for this market.

## Approved Buyer Language

Use these terms in all marketing, copy, outreach, and client-facing materials:

| Use This | Not This |
|----------|----------|
| COR-eligible | Compliance-enabled |
| Audit-ready | Compliant |
| Incident-free | Safe |
| No WCB surprises | Reduced risk |
| Clean MOC | Managed operations |
| Continuous compliance readiness | Compliance management |
| Push notification | Alert or reminder |
| Approve or Amend | Review and sign |

## Tone

**Primary:** Direct, operational, no-nonsense
**Secondary:** Confident without being aggressive
**Never:** Corporate jargon, tech-company enthusiasm, motivational language, buzzwords

**Write like:** A senior safety advisor explaining something to a site manager — clear, specific, assumes competence, respects the person's time.

**Avoid:**
- "Leverage" as a verb
- "Seamlessly"
- "Best-in-class"
- "End-to-end solution"
- "Empower"
- "Transform your compliance"
- "AI-powered" as the lead — lead with the outcome, not the technology

---

## Messaging Hierarchy

### Headline level (one sentence)
Lead with the outcome the buyer fears losing:
> "Your COR doesn't lapse. Your certs don't expire unnoticed. Your paperwork is right."

### Supporting level (two to three sentences)
Explain the mechanism without jargon:
> "Vaquero monitors every certification, regulation change, and inspection deadline across your operation. When something needs action, you get a notification with one button — Approve or Amend. No portal. No login. No chasing."

### Proof level (specific, not generic)
Use concrete compliance facts, not vague claims:
> "90-day alert ladder. Automatic DocuSign routing. Signed documents archived to your SharePoint the moment the envelope closes. COR evidence package generated on demand."

## Competitive Framing

When comparing to pull-portal competitors (ISNetworld, Avetta, Veriforce, ComplyWorks):

- Never attack by name in public-facing content
- Frame the contrast around buyer experience: "You don't log in. We push to you."
- Financial contrast when relevant: ISNetworld charges $1,000+/vendor/year — reference as "per-vendor subscription fees" without naming

---

## What Vaquero Is Not

Never position Vaquero as:
- An HR platform
- A training delivery system
- A cybersecurity tool
- A general document management system
- An AI chatbot or assistant
- A replacement for a safety advisor

Vaquero is the operational layer that makes the safety advisor's work scalable and auditable.

## Industries — Voice Nuance

| Industry | Specific Language |
|---|---|
| Oil & Gas | COR, ESC, AER, CVIP, H2S, confined space, well site, LEED, ground disturbance |
| Construction | COR, ACSA, JHA, fall protection, site orientation, subcontractor management |
| Logging / Forestry | Faller certification, equipment inspection, remote site compliance, chain of custody |
| Commercial Trucking | NSC, HOS, ELD, CVIP, trip inspection, driver abstract, dangerous goods (TDG) |

Match the buyer's vocabulary. Don't use O&G language when writing for a trucking audience.

## Industry Scope

**Commercial Trucking** for this matrix means:
- Intra-provincial carriers (regulated provincially)
- Interprovincial carriers (federally regulated under MVTA)
- For-hire and private carriers
- Flatdeck, tanker, dry van, refrigerated, oversized/overweight (OW/OS), lowbed
- Oilfield service vehicles (pump trucks, wireline, coil tubing — see ELD exemption)
- Dangerous goods carriers (TDG-regulated)
- Last-mile / urban delivery (where applicable to NSC requirements)
- Owner-operators (both as employers and as workers)

**Trucking has a uniquely split regulatory architecture.** Unlike other industries where provincial OHS is primary, trucking has:
- **Federal**: Motor Vehicle Transport Act, NSC Standards, HOS Regulations, ELD mandate, TDG, CVSA
- **Provincial OHS**: Applies to the worker (driver) in the cab — loading/unloading, pre-trip inspection
- **Provincial highway traffic**: CDL licensing, vehicle registration, overweight permits, CVIP
- All three layers apply simultaneously.

## Industry Scope

**Logging / Forestry** for this matrix means:
- Harvesting operations: fallers, buckers, chokermen, skidder/forwarder operators
- Log hauling (in-woods and highway — see Trucking matrix for highway component)
- Silviculture: planting, brushing/weeding, spacing, cone collection
- Log sort and reload yards
- Sawmill and primary wood processing (where distinct from manufacturing)
- Christmas tree farming / short rotation forestry
- Aerial operations: helicopter logging, aerial spraying
- Forest firefighting (private contractor crews)

**Provincial variation is extreme in this industry.** BC has the most developed forestry-specific OHS regime in Canada. AB, ON, and QC have meaningful forestry sectors with distinct regulations. Atlantic provinces have smaller but active industries.

### BC Part 26 — Specific Scope to Document
BC OHS Regulation Part 26 covers these distinct operations — each may have different credential requirements:
- Hand falling (chainsaw)
- Mechanical falling (feller buncher, harvester)
- Yarding (cable logging: tower, grapple, swing)
- Loading (knuckleboom, heel boom)
- Road construction (in-forest)
- Silviculture (planting, brushing)

## Section C — Federal Overlay: Logging-Specific

### Migratory Birds Convention Act (MBCA) — Schedule Cutblocks
- Federal prohibition on disturbing active migratory bird nests
- Timing windows for harvesting near nesting habitat vary by species and region
- Not an OHS credential but operationally required for harvesting authorization
- `[VERIFY_REQUIRED — current MBCA timing windows for AB/BC/ON/QC]`

### Species at Risk Act (SARA) — Harvest Restrictions
- Federal listed species may impose harvest restrictions or exclusion zones
- Environment and Climate Change Canada administers
- `[VERIFY_REQUIRED — current SARA-listed species affecting forestry in key provinces]`

### Pest Management Regulatory Agency (PMRA) — Pesticide Licensing
- Federal body (Health Canada) that registers pesticides under the Pest Control Products Act
- Provincial pesticide applicator licences are required operationally but federal registration governs products
- `[VERIFY_REQUIRED — confirm provincial pesticide applicator licence requirements per province]`

### Federal Lands Forestry
- Forestry on federal Crown lands (national parks, federal reserves) = CLC Part II applies
- Parks Canada, DND, federal Crown corporations
- `[VERIFY_REQUIRED — confirm extent of federally managed forestry in each province]`

### Carbon Credits / GGPPA — Forestry Offset
- BC and AB have forest carbon offset protocols
- Not directly an OHS instrument but increasingly operationally relevant

## Industry Scope

**Construction** for this matrix means:
- General contractors and prime contractors
- All designated trades: electrical, plumbing, HVAC/gas fitting, pipefitting, carpentry, concrete forming, ironwork/structural steel, welding, scaffolding, rigging, excavation/earthworks, roofing, drywall, masonry, insulation, glazing
- Specialty subcontractors in each trade
- Industrial construction (plant builds, turnarounds, shutdowns)
- Residential, commercial, and heavy industrial construction

### Quebec CCQ Overlay — Critical
Quebec construction is uniquely regulated. The **Commission de la construction du Québec (CCQ)** controls:
- Jurisdiction over all construction workers (who can work on construction sites)
- Trade qualifications and compagnon/apprentice ratios
- Working conditions (collective agreement — Décret)
- CCQ card required for all construction workers — not just trades

This is separate from CNESST (OHS regulator). Both apply simultaneously on QC construction sites.
